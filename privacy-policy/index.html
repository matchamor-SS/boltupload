Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026 · Last updated October 20, 2026

1. Who we are

Set & Settled ("we", "us") operates Set & Settled (the "Service"), a business-management tool for independent performing artists: bookings, contacts, templates, invoices and records. Contact: support@setandsettled.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and a securely hashed password or Google sign-in identifier.
  • Content you enter: bookings, contacts, email templates, invoices, income and expense records, kit text, and settings.
  • Google user data you authorize: described in section 4.
  • Payment usernames you choose to share (for example a Venmo or Cash App username, a PayPal link or a Zelle email) so they can appear on your invoices. The Service never stores bank account, routing or card numbers. You can type them into an invoice or email you are sending, and they are used only for that message; before anything is saved, they are removed except for the last four digits. Copies you send, or choose to save to your own Google Drive, are yours and stay in your accounts.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, and security logs, kept for security and troubleshooting.

We do not collect precise location and we do not use advertising cookies or advertising trackers.

3. How we use information

We use your information only to provide and secure the Service: to authenticate you, run the features you use, generate invoices you request, send the emails you explicitly choose to send, prevent abuse, and comply with law. We do not sell personal information.

4. Google user data: access, use, storage and deletion

The application strictly requests access to 'gmail.send' to send emails at the explicit direction of the user. We do not store, scan, read, or share user email content, and no user data is transferred to third-party tools or used for advertising/marketing models.

You connect each Google feature separately, when you first need it, and can disconnect at any time.

PermissionWhat it lets us doWhat we do not do
Gmail: send (gmail.send)Send an email from your own account only after you review the recipients, subject and message and press "Send now." This includes invoices with a PDF attachment.We never send without that click. We cannot read your inbox, sent folder, threads or drafts.

What we store. We do not store the content of your emails: not subjects, bodies, recipients' replies or attachments. When you send an email we keep only a record that a message was sent (Gmail's message identifier, the time, the booking it relates to, and whether it had an attachment) so you can see your send history. Invoice PDFs and message text pass through our servers in memory only for the moment of sending.

Tokens. Your Google refresh token is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on our servers and is never sent to your browser. Short-lived access tokens are created for each request and are not stored.

Humans do not read your data. Our staff do not view your Google data except with your affirmative agreement for a specific support request, where necessary for security or abuse investigation, or where required by law.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy: Limited Use

Set & Settled's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features described in this policy.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to others except as necessary to provide those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets after notice to you.
  • We do not use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements, including retargeting, personalized or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not allow humans to read Google user data except as described in section 4.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

6. Service providers

We do not sell or share your data for marketing. To run the Service we rely on Supabase (database hosting and authentication) and Google Cloud infrastructure. These providers process data on our behalf under contract and confidentiality. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, per-user data isolation (row-level security), least-privilege Google permissions, multi-factor authentication, access logging, and an independent annual review of our Google integration where required. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users and regulators of a breach as required by law.

8. Your choices and rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your information in Settings or by contacting support@setandsettled.com. Depending on where you live (for example California, the EEA or the UK) you may have additional rights to know, port, restrict, object and appeal. We do not discriminate against you for exercising them and will respond within the time required by law. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

9. Retention and deletion

We keep account data while your account is active. When you disconnect Google, we revoke the token with Google and delete our stored copy immediately. When you delete your account, we delete your data from active systems immediately and from encrypted backups within 30 days. We may keep minimal security and billing records for up to 12 months where required by law.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

11. Changes

We will post updates here and notify you in the app or by email before material changes take effect.
